Market

Consumer-directed healthcare is a prepaid wallet the size of a mid-size retailer, growing every year, and still shockingly bad at shopping.

The wallet. FSAs and HSAs are IRS-blessed pre-tax accounts. 2026 health FSA cap is $3,400 (carryover up to $680). HSAs are triple tax-advantaged and investable. Year-end 2025: 41.7 million HSAs holding ~$174B; 35M+ FSA holders. Combined, 70M+ Americans can spend this money.

The leak. FSA is use-it-or-lose-it (rollover or Mar 15 grace — employer picks one). Billions forfeit or get blown on last-minute eligible junk. HSA holders under-spend on care and over-save. Confusion is the competitor. Education is a product.

The aisle. Eligible goods: OTC meds, pain, skin-as-treatment, sun, diagnostics, first aid, devices, menstrual, telehealth, hearing, and a long tail of ‘surprising’ items. Health-E’s Eligibility List is a product, not a FAQ. CARES Act 2020 (OTC + menstrual without Rx) exploded the catalog.

The pipes. Third-party administrators hold the money and the login: WEX, HealthEquity, Optum, Fidelity, Lively and 250+ others. Amazon has catalog. Health-E has the benefits aisle. Jan 2026 WEX embed is the strategy in one deal.

Two banners, two jobs. FSA Store ~$122M GMV ’25, ~4.0–4.5% CVR, $125–150 AOV — deadline heat. HSA Store ~$48M, ~3.0–3.5% CVR, $150–175 AOV — relationship. Combined ~$170M. Do not average the KPIs.

Expert overnight

Health FSA. Employer-sponsored, pre-tax, use-it-or-lose-it. 2026 salary-reduction cap $3,400. Optional $680 carryover or a grace period through March 15 — employer chooses one, not both. Money is ‘use or burn.’ That is why FSA Store converts hotter and why Dec 31 / Mar 15 are merchandising Super Bowls.

HSA. Paired with a high-deductible health plan. Triple tax-advantaged: in, growth, out (for qualified spend). Rolls forever, investable. 2026 HSA limits sit on a different IRS table than FSA. Holders under-spend on care and over-save. HSA Store is a relationship and AOV business, not a panic business.

Dependent care FSA. Different animal. 2026 DCFSA cap $7,500. Daycare, not bandages. Do not mix it into FSA Store merchandising. If it comes up, show you know the split.

IIAS + substantiation. Inventory Information Approval System lets an FSA/HSA debit card auto-approve eligible SKUs at qualified merchants. Health-E’s guarantee is the consumer version of that: nothing on the shelf should fail substantiation. That is a legal product (Rachel Rouleau) and a merch constraint.

LMN. Letter of Medical Necessity. A clinician’s note that turns a ‘maybe’ (massage device, some fitness, some supplements) into eligible spend. Learning Center content + PDP education is how you convert LMN shoppers without a call center.

CARES Act (2020). OTC medicines and menstrual products became eligible without a prescription. Category explosion Health-E advocated for. If they ask ‘what changed the catalog,’ this is the answer.

Usually eligible

OTC pain, cold, allergy, digestive; Acne treatment (not cosmetic serums); Sunscreen / mineral SPF; Menstrual products; First aid, bandages, antiseptics; Thermometers, BP monitors, pulse ox, tests; Eyeglasses, contacts, reading glasses; CPAP and many sleep-therapy devices; Hearing aids (OTC era is live); Eligible telehealth (Patiently: GLP-1, menopause HRT); Many heat/cold therapy and TENS devices

Usually not

Cosmetic skincare and anti-aging as beauty; Toothpaste, mouthwash (cosmetic oral care); Most vitamins/supplements without LMN; Gym memberships without LMN; Insect repellent (classic ‘that’s covered!?’ miss); Diapers (usually no — exception paths exist, don’t guess); General wellness gadgets with no medical claim

Guaranteed eligible is the brand. I never merch a hero that would fail substantiation. Gray-area SKUs go through compliance, or they get an LMN module, not a boost.

50 bp on FSA Store. If a recent month is ~$9.1M at 4.25% CVR, a +0.50pt CVR move (to 4.75%) is ~+12% orders, holding AOV. That is on the order of ~$1M extra GMV in a similar month — tens of millions annualized if it holds. This is how you talk about the job: not ‘make the site nicer.’

$10 AOV on HSA Store. HSA AOV ~$160. +$10 at 3.25% CVR on ~688k sessions is roughly +$22k in a month from AOV alone. Attach, bundles, and device mix are the levers — not homepage poetry.

Forfeiture is demand. Unused FSA dollars are not a CSR problem. They are a merchandising calendar. Education in October, devices in November, kits in December, grace-period remaining-balance in March.
